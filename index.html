This is not your paycheck. Every figure here is pulled from a mid-month sales report and shows business as it was written, not business that has cleared. Sales fall off — cancellations, non-pays, returns, disconnects inside the guarantee window, orders that never activate, corrections from Comcast — and every one of those pulls the number down after this page was built.

The blended wage shown is the report’s own “Blended Wage — ESTIMATE ONLY” figure. It is a projection, it is not approved, and it is not a promise. Your actual pay is determined by finalized month-end results, chargebacks and fall-off, and official Blufox payroll calculations — which will differ from what you see here. Never make a financial decision on this page.

Questions about your commission go to your store manager or your DM — not to this page.
